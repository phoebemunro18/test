Where the data comes from

Decided by employees, not a judging panel

Every number in this report comes from anonymous employee responses to the Trust Index™ Employee Survey, run independently by Great Place To Work®.

Employees respond

60 statements on credibility, respect, fairness, pride and camaraderie, answered anonymously.

Organisations certify

Certification™ is earned at a 65% Trust Index score. It is the entry point to every list.

Lists are ranked
