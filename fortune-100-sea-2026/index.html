    Methodology

    How the list is ranked.

    No company can buy a place. Scores come from what employees tell us directly, and leaders only ever see grouped results.

    1. 1

      Earn Certification™.

      Every company on the list is Great Place To Work® Certified™ in at least one Southeast Asian market.

    2. 2

      Survey every employee.

      The whole workforce takes the Trust Index™ survey, frontline included. Your people answer directly to us.

    3. 3

      Rank by experience.

      Most of each score comes from what employees say. The rest comes from the Culture Audit™, our review of people programmes.

    Who is eligible.

    • Great Place To Work® Certified™ in Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand, Indonesia or Vietnam
    • 200 or more employees in Southeast Asia
    • Trust Index™ survey run between October 2025 and September 20265
    • Survey and response standards met for every market surveyed

    FAQs

    Questions about the list.

    Who can be on the Fortune 100 Best Companies to Work For Southeast Asia list?

    Any organisation that is Certified in at least one Southeast Asian market and has 200 or more employees in the region.

    How are companies ranked?

    Mostly on what employees say in the Trust Index survey, weighted so every voice counts equally. A smaller share comes from the Culture Audit, which reviews people programmes and policies.

    Are survey responses confidential?

    Yes. Employees answer directly to Great Place To Work, and leaders only see results for groups large enough to protect individual answers.

    Can my company apply for the 2027 list?

    Start by getting Certified in any Southeast Asian market. Certified companies that meet the size rule are considered automatically when the 2027 survey window closes.

    How can named companies use the badge?

    Named companies receive a media kit with badge artwork, usage rules and a press release template.
